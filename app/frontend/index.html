    Make your Bitcoin node reachable

    What this does. Other Bitcoin nodes will be able to connect to your node through your VPS. Today your node can only reach out; nothing can reach in.

    • Your home IP stays private. Peers connect to your VPS, not to you.
    • This does not make your node anonymous. Connections your node makes out still leave from your home internet connection, exactly as they do now.
    • Expect roughly 100–300 GB a month of extra VPS traffic, sometimes more when another node is syncing from you. Check your VPS plan's allowance — you can cap it, see below.
    • Incoming peers all look like one local connection to your node. That is exactly how inbound Tor already works, and is normal.
    • One manual step, once. You will paste a single line into your Bitcoin app's settings. We give you the line and a Copy button.
    • Before you uninstall HashGG or change VPS, turn this off and remove that line — otherwise your node keeps advertising a dead address.
    This needs a VPS. Your node gets its public address from one. About $5/month is plenty — it needs a dedicated IPv4 and enough transfer allowance, not a fast machine.
